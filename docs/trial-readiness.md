# Trial Readiness Notes

Use this note before inviting a small group of users to try the online demo.

## Trial Scope

- Show the online demo as a product concept, not a paid production service.
- Recommended audience: internal reviewers, trusted guardians, and small usability interviews.
- Recommended duration: 3 to 5 minutes per walkthrough.
- Recommended device check: desktop and one mobile viewport.

## Privacy Position

- Do not ask for student name, school name, region, phone number, or payment information.
- The current welcome form only asks for birth month to demonstrate age-appropriate setup.
- Demo session tokens and invite codes should not be copied into external documents.
- If a browser session is stale, use `最初からやり直します`.

## Feedback Questions

- Can the student understand what to do next from `Demo Guide`?
- Does `Quest Map` make learning progress feel visible?
- Does `冒険バッグ` make rewards understandable?
- Can the guardian understand today’s learning result from `家庭サポートメモ`?
- Is the `デモ用の回答` path clear enough for a live walkthrough?

## Known Boundaries

- Payment and subscription purchase are intentionally not enabled.
- Review quest and listening cove rewards are frontend demo-only rewards.
- Demo sessions are for walkthroughs, not long-term learning history.
- Backend cold start can still take a few seconds; the welcome page has slow-start copy.
